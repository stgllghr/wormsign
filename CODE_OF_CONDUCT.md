# Code of Conduct

wormsign adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
security advisories, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and do not harass,
demean or expose anyone. Read the Covenant itself for the full standard and the enforcement guidelines that the
maintainer follows.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, [@stgllghr](https://github.com/stgllghr), through GitHub. For
anything you would rather not raise in public, open an issue with the "Request a private contact" template,
put no details in it, and the maintainer will follow up privately. Reports are handled as confidentially as
the situation allows, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this code, and may restrict anyone from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
